Mara Vale kept the last lighthouse on the drowned coast. For years she believed she was the only living keeper left.

At dawn she found a sealed bottle on the gallery rail. Inside was a map of streets that no longer existed above the water.

By noon a stranger named Kellan arrived in a skiff and claimed the map was his. Mara did not trust him, but the tide was already turning.

Three days later the water receded far enough that they walked the old city together. Kellan pointed to an archive hall and said his order had sent him to recover what the sea had hidden.

That night Mara learned the truth: she had never been forgotten. Until then she thought solitude was her duty. After midnight she agreed to leave the lighthouse and carry the map inland.
